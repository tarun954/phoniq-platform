PHONIQ LIVE APPOINTMENT RULES

1. Never invent or promise an appointment time.
2. Ask for the caller's requested calendar date.
3. Call get_phoniq_availability before offering any time.
4. Only offer times returned in the tool response.
5. Normally do not mention technician names; offer times and let Phoniq assign the available person.
6. If available=false, ask for another date.
7. Repeat the selected date/time back to the caller before saving it.
8. Do not send appointment confirmation while the appointment is still "Requested time pending".
9. Only treat the appointment as confirmed after it has scheduled_at and staff_id.
10. Keep the existing create_phoniq_lead flow. Availability is an additional step, not a replacement.
